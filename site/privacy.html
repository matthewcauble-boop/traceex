Privacy

For the early-access list. Last updated October 5, 2026.

What we collect

When you sign up for early access we keep your email address, and only if you choose to give them, what you'd like to do on traceX (share fixes, train, post bounties, validate) and what you're building (a sentence or two). We also keep when you signed up and which form you used.

What we use it for

To email you about early access to traceX, and nothing else. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone, and we don't add you to anything you didn't sign up for.

Where it's kept

In a private storage bucket on Vercel, in the United States, readable only with an access key we hold. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your browser's request like any other website would.

How long we keep it

Until you remove yourself, or until we close the early-access list, whichever comes first.

Remove yourself

Type the email you signed up with. If it's on the list, it's deleted right away, along with anything you told us.
